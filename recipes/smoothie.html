Smmothie recipe for hot days

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Greek yogurt
  • Fruits (berries, bananas, etc)
  • Toppings (whipped cream, sprinkles)

Steps

  1. blend fruits, milk and greek yogurt together
  2. Pour in a glass
  3. Top with whipped cream and sprinkles
