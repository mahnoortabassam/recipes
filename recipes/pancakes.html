Pancakes

Description

Classic pancakes made using flour,eggs, milk and sugar.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Oil or butter

Steps

  1. Combine flour, sugar and baking powder in one bowl
  2. Combine eggs and milk in another bowl
  3. Mix the wet and dry ingredients together
  4. Add oil or butter
  5. Heat a frying pan
  6. Pour the batter in round shapes
  7. Flip when bubbles start to form on top of the pancakes
  8. Serve with toppings and enjoy!
