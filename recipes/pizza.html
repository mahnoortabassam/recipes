Pizza

Description

A classic Italian Pizza made with layers of tomato sauce, cheese, and a crispy crust.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Basil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Knead and roll the pizza dough
  2. Form a circular shape with the dough
  3. Prepare the tomato sauce or use store brought
  4. Layer ingredients: tomato sauce, cheese and basil
  5. Bake in oven
